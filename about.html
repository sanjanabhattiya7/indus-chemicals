01 / Expertise

Our Expertise

Indus Chemicals Pvt. Ltd. (ICPL) is a professionally managed organization operating since 1992. We specialize in water treatment plants, wastewater treatment plants, water treatment chemicals, plant spares, testing instruments and field support. Our experience helps us understand industrial water treatment requirements and provide suitable solutions for operational challenges.

02 / Approach

Our Approach

ICPL connects global manufacturers with end users, helping customers access suitable technologies and dependable products. We emphasize innovation, product quality and practical solutions tailored to individual requirements. Honesty, integrity, transparency and customer trust guide our work, while continuous improvement supports long-term business partnerships.

03 / Support

Complete Customer Support

Our support extends beyond product supply. We assist customers with turnkey projects, plant management, project management, on-site training, Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC), Operation and Maintenance Contracts (OMC), and troubleshooting. Our goal is to support efficient plant operations, improve reliability and help customers maintain their water treatment systems throughout their operational journey.

Our Objective
